## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: the founder of TEKGUYZ**, working TEKGUYZ's own sales pipeline —
enquiries from tekguyz.com, cold prospects, follow-ups, won and lost deals.
Accounts are invite-only: today the founder plus one or two trusted,
hand-provisioned collaborators (OWNER / ADMIN / MEMBER roles).

**Secondary: a visitor evaluating the product** through the public, read-only
`/demo` tenant, linked from the case study on tekguyz.com and from `/login`.
They can look at everything and change nothing.

**Later, not now:** other small service businesses as paying tenants. The
architecture is multi-tenant for that reason, but self-serve signup, billing
and terms do not exist and are deliberately deferred (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).

## Product Purpose

Track every lead from first enquiry to closed deal: pipeline, follow-ups and
revenue in one place. Success is that no enquiry is lost or forgotten, the
next action on every live lead is obvious, and won/lost revenue is recorded
explicitly rather than guessed.

## Positioning

Confirmed by the founder, 2026-09-25:

- **Wired to its own front door.** The tekguyz.com enquiry form posts into the
  CRM over a signed webhook (per-tenant HMAC, replay and rate-limit guards), so
  a lead lands in the pipeline with no retyping.
- **Nothing is lost.** No hard deletes — removal is `archived`. A returning
  archived client is resurrected as NEW and marked `[Returned Prospect]`.
  Automated judgement (the AI spam shield, any future scoring) routes a lead;
  it never hides one or silences its notification.
- **Small and fast.** Built for how a small service business actually sells —
  enquiry, follow-up, won — and nothing an enterprise CRM would add on top.
- **Owned, not rented.** No per-seat SaaS fee; TEKGUYZ owns the code and the
  data.

## Operating Context

- **Mostly desktop or laptop**, working the pipeline at a desk. Phone or tablet
  when out: react to a notification, then one-tap call, text, email or map.
- **The phone experience is currently poor** — the founder has avoided using it
  on a phone because of how it looks. Mobile is a real use scene that the
  product does not yet serve well; do not treat it as out of scope.
- Leads enter three ways: the signed webhook from tekguyz.com, manual entry,
  and CSV import into Prospects (cold-outreach staging) with an explicit
  promote-to-lead step.
- Daily rhythm lives on Today, Pipeline (kanban by stage), Contacts, Prospects
  and Reports, with a command palette for global search.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Shipped:** leads with pipeline stages and SLA "Going Cold" flags; tasks;
  contacts directory; prospects with import and one-transaction promotion;
  reports by period with CSV export; team management and per-lead ownership;
  help drawer; command palette; idle session timeout; read-only public demo.
- **Terminology:** *lead* (a live sales record), *prospect* (cold-outreach
  staging, not yet a lead), *contact* (every non-archived lead, any outcome),
  *outcome* (WON / LOST / ABANDONED), *Going Cold* (SLA breach),
  *Returned Prospect* (a resurrected archived lead).
- **Tenant isolation is Postgres RLS**, not application code. Security rules
  are permanent: `docs/SECURITY_MODEL.md`.
- **Click-to-action:** every phone renders `tel:` and `sms:`, every email
  `mailto:`, every address a Maps link.
- **Undecided / future:** integrations and connectors beyond the tekguyz.com
  webhook (direction wanted, nothing specified); notifications, PWA push
  (offline declined), lead enrichment with a human-apply step, 2FA — see
  `docs/ROADMAP.md`. Onboarding is deferred until a real invite is imminent.
  A marketing landing page is deferred until self-serve signup exists.

## Product Principles

1. **Never lose a lead.** Every automated path routes or flags; only a human
   removes.
2. **The next action is obvious.** Every screen answers "what do I do next on
   this lead" before it shows anything else.
3. **Record the truth, don't estimate it.** Outcome and revenue are explicit
   fields a human sets.
4. **Earn every feature.** Build for a small service business's real work; a
   capability without a real user asking for it waits on the roadmap.
5. **Owned end to end.** Prefer what TEKGUYZ controls — its own site, its own
   data, its own code — over a rented dependency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
